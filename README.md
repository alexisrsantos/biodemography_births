# Accelerated fertility decline following the onset of the economic crisis in Puerto Rico, 1990–2020
This repository contains data and code needed to replicate the analysis presented in the article
> Santos-Lozada, A. R., Delucca-Morales, R. M., & Fuentes, L. (2026). Accelerated fertility decline following the onset of the economic crisis in Puerto Rico, 1990–2020. Biodemography and Social Biology, 1–15. https://doi.org/10.1080/19485565.2026.2716716

In this repository you will find the [code](/Replication_CodeGitHub.R) and [data](/Data_fecun.xlsx) needed to replicate our analysis. 

## Codebook
The data includes the following variables:
* Year = Year of Observation
* Nacimientos = Total Births
* Population = Population Estimate (or census)
* CBR = Crude Birth Rate per 1,000 people
* log_CBR = log scale CBR
* ID = Number of Observation-Year
* Crisis = Dummy with 2005 as onset of crisis (used for Supplementary)
* Crisis 2 = Dummy with 2006 as onset of crisis (used in Main results)
* pop15_19 = Population Estimate 15-19 years
* pop20_24 = Population Estimate 20-24 years
* pop25_29 = Population Estimate 25-29 years
* pop30_34 = Population Estimate 30-34 years
* pop35_39 = Population Estimate 35-39 years
* pop40_44 = Population Estimate 40-44 years
* pop15_44 = Population Estimate 15-44 years (Sum)
* porctotal15_44 = % of the population aged 15-44 years
* log15_44 = log-scale % of population aged 15-44 years
* nac15_19 = Births to mothers 15-19 years
* nac20_24 = Births to mothers 15-19 years
* nac25_29 = Births to mothers 25-29 years
* nac30_34 = Births to mothers 30-34 years
* nac35_39 = Births to mothers 35-39 years
* nac40_44 = Births to mothers 40-44 years
* nac_residual= Births to mothers over 44 years (not used)
* asfr15_19 = ASFR for 15-19 years
* asfr20_24 = ASFR for 20-24 years
* asfr25_29 = ASFR for 25-29 years
* asfr30_34 = ASFR for 30-34 years
* asfr35_39 = ASFR for 35-39 years
* asfr40_44 = ASFR for 40-44 years
* TFR = Total Fertility Rate
* log_TFR=log of the TFR
* log_asfr15_19 = log of ASFR for 15-19 
* log_asfr20_24 = log of  ASFR for 20-24 
* log_asfr25_29 = log of  ASFR for 25-29 
* log_asfr30_34 = log of  ASFR for 30-34 
* log_asfr35_39 = log of  ASFR for 35-39 
* log_asfr40_44 = log of  ASFR for 40-44
